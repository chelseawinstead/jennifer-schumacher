About this home

Contemporary desert luxury, gated in Silverleaf.

Set behind the guarded gates of Silverleaf, this soft-contemporary estate blends warm stone, walls of glass, and seamless indoor-outdoor living. Floor-to-ceiling sliders open the great room to a resort backyard with a negative-edge pool framed by uninterrupted McDowell Mountain views.

The chef's kitchen features dual islands, integrated appliances, and a temperature-controlled wine room. A private primary wing, detached casita, and oversized four-car garage complete an offering built for effortless entertaining.
