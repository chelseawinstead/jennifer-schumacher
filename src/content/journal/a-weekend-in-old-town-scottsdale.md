Scottsdale is where desert beauty meets effortless luxury, with mountain views, world-class resorts, incredible dining, golf, shopping, art, and miles of trails all wrapped into one city. It's polished without feeling pretentious, active without feeling rushed, and one of those places where the lifestyle has a way of convincing you to stay.

Whether you're visiting for the weekend or considering calling Scottsdale home, here's where I'd point you.

## Morning

Start with coffee and breakfast in Old Town, then take advantage of the cooler morning air with a hike at McDowell Sonoran Preserve or Camelback Mountain. If hiking isn't your thing, wander through Old Town's galleries, boutiques, and local shops before the day gets moving.

**Tip:** In Scottsdale, early mornings are worth it, especially during the warmer months. The desert at sunrise is hard to beat.

## Afternoon

Lunch on a patio is practically a Scottsdale tradition. From Old Town to Kierland and North Scottsdale, there's no shortage of beautiful spots to settle in for a long lunch. Spend the afternoon shopping at Scottsdale Fashion Square, exploring the galleries and design districts, relaxing poolside at one of the resorts, or fitting in a round of golf.

This is Scottsdale, there's absolutely no pressure to choose just one.

## Evening

Scottsdale really comes alive at sunset. Start with cocktails overlooking the desert or mountains, then head to dinner at one of the city's standout restaurants. Old Town offers plenty of energy, while North Scottsdale delivers a little more space, incredible views, and that unmistakable desert-at-night feeling.

Finish with a nightcap, live music, or simply a walk under the palms and desert sky.

And if somewhere between the sunrise hike, patio lunch, and sunset dinner you start wondering what it would be like to live here?

That's usually when my phone rings.
