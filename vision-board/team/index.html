Assigned seating

Who can be seated follows each session’s “Who sees this” boxes in Event agenda (paid guests, local guests, or both). Pick a session, set up its tables, then seat people: tap a name on the right, then tap an empty seat (or tap an empty seat and choose someone). Tap a filled seat to move or remove that person. Guests see their table under that session in their app once you save.

Chat management

Everything guests have posted in the app's Chat tab, newest first. Remove anything that shouldn't be there; it disappears for everyone right away. Guests can also delete their own posts.

Agenda

Check-in

Two check-ins: Friday arrival (agents get their name tag) and the Vision Board Party (agents + locals get their gift bag). Volunteers can also check anyone in by name.
